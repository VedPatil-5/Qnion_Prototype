# Qnion product requirements

## 1. Product summary

Qnion is a prototype interface for AI-powered onion crate and batch grading at procurement centres (SIH 2026). It demonstrates how procurement staff or quality officers can evaluate a crate or heap image and obtain a structured batch quality profile containing bulb classifications, Grade A / URS / Rejected breakdowns, defect findings, and a downloadable PDF dossier.

## 2. Users and needs

### Procurement Staff
Needs a simple, guided way to scan onion batches, see defect markers in plain language, understand the Grade A / URS breakdown, and download a summary report.

### Quality Officer
Needs a dashboard with batch inspection statistics, quality defect breakdowns, sample analytics, and access to the batch scanner.

## 3. Goals

- Demonstrate a complete batch scan-to-report journey with deterministic local data.
- Demonstrate an officer workspace for reviewing procurement centre batch analytics.
- Make the distinction between Grade A, URS (under-size/sortable), and Rejected clear.
- Support English, Hindi, and Marathi UI copy.
- Keep light and dark modes readable and styled in a warm onion palette.

## 4. Non-goals

- Production computer vision server backend.
- Binding government certifications or official enforcement rulings.
- Production identity management.
